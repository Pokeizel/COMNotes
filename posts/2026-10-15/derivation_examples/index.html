Sentential Forms

Definitions

Let G be a Context-Free Grammar where G = (V, Σ, P, S).
  1. The string w ∈ (V ∪ Σ)* is a sentential form of G if, and only if, S ⇒* w.
  2. The string w ∈ Σ* is a sentence of G if, and only if, S ⇒* w.
  3. The language of G (denoted L(G)) is the set
    {v | v ∈ Σ* ^ S ⇒* w }.
  4. The language of G is the set of all sentences of G.

Example

If the context-free grammar G is...

E → E + E
E → E * E
E → a
E → b

then its sentential forms are...

{E, E + E, E + E * E, E * E, a, a + a, b, ...}

and the language of G is...

{a, b, a + a, a + b, a * a, b + a * b, b + b + b + b + b + b, ...}
