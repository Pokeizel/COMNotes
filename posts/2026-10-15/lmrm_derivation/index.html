Leftmost-Rightmost Derivation

Definitions

A derivation is leftmost ("más izquierda", in case that's used in the exam to throw someone off) if the non-terminal closest to the start of the string is always replaced.

A derivation is rightmost ("más derecha", in case that's used in the exam to throw someone off) if the non-terminal closest to the end of the string is always replaced.

A set of strings is a context-free language if it's completely generated by a context-free grammar G and G doesn't generate strings that do not belong to the language.

Leftmost Example


E
⇒ E * E
⇒ E + E * E
⇒ b + E * E
⇒ b + E * E * E
⇒ b + a * E * E
⇒ b + a * a * E
⇒ b + a * a * b

Rightmost Example

E
⇒ E + E
⇒ E + E * E
⇒ E + E * E + E
⇒ E + E * E + b
⇒ E + E * E + E + b
⇒ E + E * E + b + b
⇒ E + E * a + b + b
⇒ E + a * a + b + b
⇒ b + a * a + b + b
