Examples of Finite Automata to Regular Grammar Algorithm

Examples


DFA for a language over Σ = {0, 1} of strings ending in 0. The algorithm turns it into this regular grammar:

A → 1A | 0B
B → 0B | 1A | ε


DFA for a language over Σ = {0, 1} of strings not ending in 00. The algorithm turns it into this regular grammar:

A → 1A | 0B | ε
B → 0C | 1A | ε B → 0C | 1A


DFA for a language over Σ = {G, C, A, T} of strings not containing the substring "GAGA". The algorithm turns it into this regular grammar:

S → GP | CS | AS | TS | ε
P → GP | CS | AQ | TS | ε
Q → GR | CS | AS | TS | ε
R → GP | TS | CS | ε
